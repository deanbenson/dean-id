How we work.

01

Nothing off the shelf.

Every install starts from a blank page: your venue, your palette, your moment. If you have seen it at someone else's party, it is not coming to yours.

( 10 — Good questions )

Before you ask.

How far ahead should we book?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for most parties, and larger events like weddings deserve a couple of months. Something sooner? Message us anyway, we love a challenge.

Do you deliver and set up?

Always. Every collection includes delivery, install and styling, and most include the strike too. You will never be handed a bin bag and wished good luck.

Which areas do you cover?

Hull and everywhere around it: Beverley, Cottingham, Hessle, Willerby, Anlaby, Kirk Ella, Hedon, Brough and across the East Riding. Further afield is usually easy to sort.

Can you match a theme or palette?

That is the fun bit. Send a Pinterest board, a screenshot, or three words, and we will design around it. Any theme, any palette, from barely-there neutrals to full technicolour.

What happens to everything afterwards?

We come back and strike it all. Latex balloons are biodegradable, foils are bagged for reuse where possible, and frames and props live to party again.

Do you take a deposit?

A small deposit holds your date, with the balance due on the day once you have seen the room and gasped appropriately.
