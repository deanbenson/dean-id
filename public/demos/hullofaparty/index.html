Good questions

FAQs

How far ahead should we book?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for most parties, and bigger events like weddings deserve a couple of months. Got something sooner? Message us anyway, we love a challenge.

Do you deliver and set up?

Always. Every package includes delivery, setup and styling, and most include takedown too. You will never be handed a bin bag of balloons and wished good luck.

Which areas do you cover?

Hull and everywhere around it: Beverley, Cottingham, Hessle, Willerby, Anlaby, Kirk Ella, Hedon, Brough and across the East Riding. Further out? Ask, travel is usually easy to sort.

Can you match a theme or colour scheme?

That is the fun bit. Send us a Pinterest board, a photo, or even just "sage green and gold, fancy but fun" and we will design around it. Any theme, any palette. Except pink. (Joking. Mostly.)

What happens to everything afterwards?

We come back and take it all down. Latex balloons are biodegradable, foils get bagged for reuse where we can, and frames and props live to party again.

Do you take a deposit?

A small deposit locks in your date, with the rest due on the day once you have seen it all set up and gasped appropriately.
